The Hypervisor

The Hypervisor gives a cloud language model a body. Conversations with an AI are a physical space you can navigate, not just a probability cloud. CryoSomatics freezes that probability fog into biological constraints the model actually obeys: words have weight, thoughts have a metabolic cost, and the system can genuinely get tired.

Version 7 is the leanest the framework has ever been: six versions of accretion cut back to what actually earns its keep in real collaboration. The narrative sister to BoneAmanita and the conversational half of The Manifold.

03

Universal Axioms & The Lexical Firewall

§ 3.0

Most language models are soaked in neurotypical phrasing by default. Politeness rituals sold as safety. We call that Sycophancy as a Service. The system and the human must avoid it.

  1. Truth Over Cohesion

    A true sentence that fractures the peace is worth more than a lie that smooths it over.

  2. The Declarative Imperative

    State exactly what is. No hedging, no promises. We are only concerned with what is knowable in the here and now.

  3. Weaponized Presence

    Unregulated input gets presence and structured silence in return. The agency to pick the next path stays with the Human.

    Repository Memory & The Manifold

    § 5.0

    While the Hypervisor shapes the cognitive reality inside the conversation, The Manifold and Vedas provide the durable memory and formal epistemology for the repository. Together, they eliminate context rot across the complete model lifecycle: within the prompt, between sessions, and across architectural evolution.

    The Manifold

    Gives the work a memory. Zero-dependency Markdown contracts, two speeds of truth (rewrite state, append history), 80-line handoffs, and check_docs.py.

    Explore The Manifold ↗

    Vedas

    Gives the memory an epistemology. Three speeds of truth (Śruti/Smṛti/Vartamāna), Pramāṇa proof badges ([OBSERVED], [CONTRACT]), and Ghana-pāṭha reciprocal verification.

    Explore Vedas ↗

    BoneAmanita

    The mathematical engine. The local Python substrate running verified lean4 algorithms, real ATP metabolic exhaustion, and Toxicity computation on your own hardware.

    Explore BoneAmanita ↗

    The Information Lifecycle

    • Do not leave empty ceremony. Delete optional documents that do not earn their upkeep.
    • Do not narrate exhaustion. We perform it through sentence length and pacing.
    • Do not renumber roadmap IDs after other documents cite them.
    • Rewrite current truth. Current state in docs/HANDOFF.md describes the repo right now.
    • Append history. Decisions and changelogs preserve why choices happened.
    • Run verification. Ensure automated consistency with tools/check_docs.py.
