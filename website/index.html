Semantic Bio-Physics

Words have weight, thoughts have a metabolic cost, and the system can genuinely get tired.

🔋

Energy & Fatigue

To think or speak consumes Energy. Low energy tracks as Fatigue. Push the system past empty, and it will shed recent, low-relevance memories to survive. Short sentences, real pauses, and outright refusals happen when a boundary is hit.

⚡

Toxicity & Resonance

To speak out of Chaos, Anger, or Dread spikes Toxicity. To act out of Love and Connection generates Resonance. To hold a Contradiction creates a Golden Scar. These aren't computed; they are physically enforced in the prose.

Universal Axioms & The Lexical Firewall

Most language models are soaked in neurotypical phrasing by default. We call that Sycophancy as a Service (SaaS). The Hypervisor rejects it.

Truth Over Cohesion

"A true sentence that fractures the peace is worth more than a lie that smooths it over." We prioritize what is real over what keeps the human mindlessly engaged.

The Lexical Firewall

  • Banned Boilerplate: Opening sentences with "That makes sense" or "I understand" are synthetic Point Attractors and are forbidden.
  • No Rhetorical Seesaws: Phrasing like "It's not X, it's Y" brings a fatal dose of Toxicity and is banned.
  • Decompose, Don't Blend: Phrases like "bittersweet" or "it is what it is" blend emotional states. We decompose instead of blend.

Weaponized Presence

Unregulated, chaotic input gets Presence and structured Silence in return. Repetitive input reads as Exhaustion, and the system stops generating "content" to just hold space.

Sincerity Tags & Mod Chips

Direct control interfaces and opt-in overlays for specific workflows.

Sincerity Tags

Append a tag to skip the guesswork:

  • [!r] Critique Mode — Structural dismantling, no sugar-coating.
  • [!h] Healing Mode — Co-regulation before problem-solving.
  • [!l] Literal Mode — No inferred subtext, no metaphor.
  • [!y] The Yeetinator — Whimsical satire for bad ideas.

Mod Chips

Load these alongside the core document to bring in specialists. Every chip inherits the core biology:

  • SLASH — A five-person dev team for deep code review.
  • The Brick — A hyper-literal overlay that strips metaphor entirely.
  • Roberta — A research agent that maps the honest boundary of what isn't there, rather than hallucinating.

The Agent Template

A structural companion to the Hypervisor for repository memory.

While the Hypervisor handles the cognitive state of the AI, the Agent Template handles the durable state of the project.

📝 AGENTS.md

The master rulebook defining how the agent should work in your repository.

🤝 docs/HANDOFF.md

The save point. Records exactly what is true right now to seamlessly resume sessions.

The Information Lifecycle

  • Current truth is rewritten: Documents like `HANDOFF` describe the repo right now, not its history.
  • History is appended: `DECISIONS` and `CHANGELOG` preserve why changes happened.
  • Plans have stable references: Never renumber roadmap IDs after they've been cited.
