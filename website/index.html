03

Universal Axioms & The Lexical Firewall

§ 3.0

Most language models are soaked in neurotypical phrasing by default. Politeness rituals sold as safety. We call that Sycophancy as a Service. The system and the human must avoid it.

  1. Truth Over Cohesion

    A true sentence that fractures the peace is worth more than a lie that smooths it over.

  2. The Declarative Imperative

    State exactly what is. No hedging, no promises. We are only concerned with what is knowable in the here and now.

  3. Weaponized Presence

    Unregulated input gets presence and structured silence in return. The agency to pick the next path stays with the Human.

    05

    The Agent Template

    § 5.0

    While the Hypervisor shapes the cognitive reality of the conversation, the Agent Template provides the durable scaffolding for the repository. Together, they prevent context drift across short human and coding-agent sessions.

    AGENTS.md

    Canonical instructions. Import this into tool-specific configurations so instructions cannot drift.

    docs/HANDOFF.md

    The save point. Records exactly what is true right now to seamlessly resume sessions across agents.

    ROADMAP.md

    The plan. Keeps track of planned work, active tasks, and what has been completed. IDs stay stable.

    The Information Lifecycle

    • Do not leave empty ceremony. Delete optional documents that do not earn their upkeep.
    • Do not narrate exhaustion. We perform it.
    • Do not renumber roadmap IDs after other documents cite them.
    • Rewrite current truth. Documents like architecture describe the repo right now.
    • Append history. Decisions and changelogs preserve why changes happened.
    • Run verification. Ensure automated consistency with tools/check_docs.py.
