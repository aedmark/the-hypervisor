# Archive

Historical session and roadmap material moves here when live documents become difficult to scan. Archived content remains searchable and non-normative. Never move secrets or sensitive incident evidence here.

- After ten handoff session entries, move the oldest complete entries into a dated `SESSION_LOG_YYYY_MM.md` file.
- Finished roadmap phases may move into dated roadmap archives without changing their permanent IDs.
- Leave a pointer in the live document whenever content moves.
